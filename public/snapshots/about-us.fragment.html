About Us

EO2 EXP is a multi-disciplinary creative studio. We work with brands, agencies, and creative co-conspirators to make good shit happen. Every project is weaved to optimally communicate the brand ethos and increase visibility through advertising, films, events, and virtual content.
With more than 20,000 projects under our belt and ongoing retainers with numerous reputed clients, EO2 has carved a niche for itself in the Marketing Industry. Now that you know the goods, check out the goods to back them up.
Our Founders
Vandana Agrawal
Founder @ EO2 EXP
EO2 EXP is the brainchild of founder and ship leader, Vandana Agrawal, who recognised the need for model coordination and fashion show organisers in the market back in 1993! Being a motivated and determined entrepreneur, she took the company from strength to strength over the next few years. With creating fashion shows across the country and internationally, EO2 EXP worked with the top designers in the country, including the likes of Manish Malhotra and Rajesh Singh. What started as a fashion show-based successful business then transformed into an events-based business that is now the bandwidth and power of an in-house strength of 35+ personnel and an alliance strength of 300+ personnel across events, experiential design and film.
Risheeta Agrawal
Co-Founder | Producer
Rishabh and Risheeta, the next generation, always showed a keen interest in the business from a very young age. Rishabh designed his first set at the age of 8 for a fashion show celebrating 50 years of Independence in 1997. While Risheeta was pitching event decks since she was 14. This interest in the business and industry shaped their respective career paths. Rishabh went on to study further in Italy and Risheeta found her calling in films. She pursued further education at Prague Film School, which ultimately led to the birth of EO2 EXP Films. Since then, our company has built a strong reputation for reliability and quality work, cultivating enduring relationships with global brands such as Pernod Ricard, L'Oreal, ASUS, Netflix, and Amazon Prime. Our extensive clientele, including prominent names in the industry, attests to our credibility and showcases our ability to consistently deliver exceptional results.
