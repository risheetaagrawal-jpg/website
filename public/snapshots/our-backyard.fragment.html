NAUGHTY AMELIA JANE
Directed by Risheeta Agrawal, an award winning short screened across 100 international film festivals. This film is a satire on the hypocrisy and blatant ignorance of society towards people and issues that are against their "social order of things", mainly the LGBT community, told through the story of two young girls who were in love.
BAR TALK WITH RAGHAV MEATTLE
Shot entirely in the lockdown, breaking the taboo against the LGBTQ community, this music video highlights tender moments from same sex relationships. Featuring Sushant Divgikr, one of India's top drag performers, the video got rave reviews from LGBTQ publications as well as other cultural publications.
PERIOD PARTY 2.0
On World Menstrual Hygiene Day, Period Party 2.0 , a 2 day online event - a collaborative initiative was hosted, in partnership with Redisthenewgreen to ensure that everyone has access to a safe and healthy period during this pandemic! We helped raise over 18 Lacs for the cause and made sure we do good, and have a party while we’re at it :)
PARCHAYI
3 years since homosexuality was decriminalized in India, but has love truly broken the shackles of society? Asking this question, our music video directed by Gaurav Gupta, explores the young love of two guys, somewhere in rural India, where it is not acceptable by the society.
