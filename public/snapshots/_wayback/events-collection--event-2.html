NRI of the Year
Eo2 organised Times Now's NRI of the Year, a prestigious event, that serves as a platform to acknowledge the remarkable accomplishments of NRIs in various fields such as business, arts, technology, philanthropy, and more. Through a rigorous selection process, exceptional individuals who have made a significant impact in their respective fields and have also contributed to India's growth and image on the global stage are recognized and awarded.
